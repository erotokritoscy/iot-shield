# ADR 0001: Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-07-07 · Status: accepted

## Context
The internship plan requires an architecture decision log. Decisions made ad hoc get
forgotten and re-litigated; the final report needs their rationale.

## Decision
Every architecture-level decision gets a numbered, append-only ADR in this directory:
Context / Decision / Consequences. Contract changes after the v1 freeze *require* an ADR.

## Consequences
Small writing overhead per decision; in exchange, the decision log deliverable writes
itself and agents/collaborators can see why things are the way they are.
